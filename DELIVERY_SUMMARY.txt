     - compute_brier_score()

ENSEMBLE CONFIGURATION:
  5 Models with seeds: [42, 123, 456, 789, 999]
  Same hyperparameters, different random initialization

ENSEMBLE METHODS:
  1. Simple average: mean([p1, p2, p3, p4, p5])
  2. Weighted average: Σ(weight_i × p_i), weight by individual AUC

STABILITY METRICS:
  - Agreement rate: % samples all models agree (target: >85%)
  - Confident predictions: std < 0.05 (target: >60%)
  - Uncertain predictions: std >= 0.05
  - ECE: Ensemble calibration error

KEY OUTPUTS:
  - ensemble_predictions_simple.npy
  - ensemble_predictions_weighted.npy
  - Stability metrics per sample
  - Uncertainty estimates

BENEFIT: Quantifies prediction confidence, improves robustness

SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Low baseline AUC (<0.65)?
  → Check data quality in Part 1
  → Verify features are numeric and clean

High data leakage score (>50)?
  → Check SMOTE is applied WITHIN folds only
  → Verify statistics computed per fold

FN rate too high (>15%)?
  → Lower classification threshold (0.25-0.30)
  → Use SMOTE strategy (higher recall)

Ensemble not improving?
  → Try weighted average instead of simple
  → Check individual models are diverse

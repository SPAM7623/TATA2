APPENDIX: METRIC DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

ROC-AUC (Area Under Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve):
  Measures ranking ability across all thresholds. Unaffected by threshold.
  Range: 0.0 (worst) to 1.0 (perfect). Higher is better.

PR-AUC (Area Under Precision-Recall Curve):
  Measures precision-recall trade-off. Sensitive to imbalance.
  Range: 0.0 to 1.0. Higher is better.

MCC (Matthews Correlation Coefficient):
  Balanced measure of classification quality. Ranges from -1 to +1.
  Accounts for true/false positives/negatives. Higher is better.

F1 Score:
  Harmonic mean of precision and recall.
  Range: 0.0 to 1.0. Higher is better.

Balanced Accuracy:
  Average of recall (TPR) and specificity (TNR).
  Fair to both classes. Range: 0.0 to 1.0. Higher is better.

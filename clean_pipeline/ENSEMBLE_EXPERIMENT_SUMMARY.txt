================================================================================
WHY KEEP 5-SEED?
================================================================================

1. PRIMARY METRIC INTEGRITY
   The 10-seed ensemble performs WORSE on ROC-AUC (0.8658 vs 0.8668),
   which is the standard ranking metric for imbalanced classification.

2. CONVERGENCE SIGNAL
   The 0.9991 prediction correlation indicates the 5-seed ensemble has
   already converged to optimal behavior. Additional seeds add noise, not signal.

3. COST-BENEFIT FAILURE
   2x training cost for -0.0010 ROC-AUC + marginal secondary gains is NOT
   justified. Simple models that work well are preferable.

4. PRACTICAL IDENTITY
   In production, both models make virtually the same predictions. The
   switching cost exceeds any benefit.

5. OVERFITTING INDICATION
   ROC-AUC decreasing with more seeds suggests the 10-seed ensemble is
   overfitting to the specific random seed variation.
